# LorraineWebsite : Web Development Agency

**Live Demo:** **[View Project](#)**

---

## Overview

As I learned front end development, I found myself imagining websites for almost every business I came across. I challenged myself to build one as if I were working with a real client, creating a professional website that clearly communicates services & showcases previous work.

---

## The Problem

Businesses have no central place where potential clients can learn about their services, view work, understand pricing, and decide whether they're the right fit.

---

## The Solution

LorraineWebsite brings these elements together in a single, professional website, creating a clear journey from discovering the business to making an enquiry.

---

## How It Works

```text
Visit the Website
       ↓
Explore Services
       ↓
Browse the Portfolio
       ↓
Learn the Development Process
       ↓
Choose a Pricing Plan
       ↓
Submit an Enquiry
```
